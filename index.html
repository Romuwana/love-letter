Chapter I: Why you

There is rather a lot
to admire about you.

I could list the obvious things first, your beauty, your smile, your eyes, but that would only describe the surface. What fascinates me is everything beneath it.

01

Your smile.

There is something remarkably disarming about it. You can turn an ordinary moment into something I want to remember simply by smiling.

02

Your mind.

I love the way you think, the way you express yourself, and the little perspectives you bring into conversations. You make me curious.

03

Your presence.

Some people enter a room and simply occupy it. You have the rather unfair ability to make the room feel different merely by being there.

04

The woman you are.

Beyond appearances, I admire your individuality. You are beautifully yourself, and I hope you never feel the need to become anything else for anyone.

My dearest Sebongile,

If I am being entirely honest, I am not sure a website can adequately express what I feel for you. Words, for all their elegance, have their limitations.

Nevertheless, I wanted to try.

I care about you more deeply than I sometimes know how to articulate. I care about your happiness, your peace, your dreams, and even the little things you might think nobody notices.

I love the way you have gradually become someone whose presence matters to me, someone I look forward to hearing from, someone whose smile can alter the atmosphere of an otherwise ordinary day.

And yes, you are making this unnecessarily difficult by playing hard to get.

But perhaps that is part of your charm. You make me work for the privilege of being close to you, and somehow I find that I do not mind the effort.

I do not want something temporary with you. I want something sincere. Something that can survive ordinary days, difficult conversations, laughter, growth, ambition and everything else that life decides to place before us.

I want to know you more deeply. I want to support you. I want to celebrate your victories. I want to be there when things are difficult. And, selfishly, I want to be the person who gets to witness that beautiful smile for a very long time.

So, Sebo, this is my rather elaborate way of saying something remarkably simple:

I choose you.

— Nathaniel ♡
Chapter IV — Perhaps...

What if this is only
the beginning?

Imagine the conversations we have yet to have. The places we have yet to visit. The photographs we have yet to take. The arguments we will eventually laugh about. The dreams we will encourage each other to pursue.

I do not know exactly what the future holds. But I know who I would like beside me while we discover it.

One last thing, astra...

Will you
be mine?

I have said what my heart wanted to say.
Now I would very much like to hear what yours says.
